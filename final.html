Recipe Details

Step-by-step guides and delicious configurations for your meal!

Blueberry Protein Smoothie

A healthy and energetic breakfast smoothie packed with wild forest blueberries and Greek yogurt.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup Fresh/Frozen Blueberries
  • 1 scoop Vanilla Protein Powder
  • 1/2 cup Greek Yogurt & Almond Milk

Sri Lankan Milk Rice (Kiribath)

Traditional coconut milk rice cooked during special celebrations and traditional tomorrows.

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups White Raw Rice
  • 2 cups Thick Coconut Milk
  • Salt to taste

Traditional Rice & Chicken Curry Platter

A grand meal platter featuring hot rice, rich chicken curry, dhal curry, and fresh sambol.

Ingredients:

  • Steamed Samba Rice
  • Spicy Roasted Chicken Gravy
  • Coconut Dhal Curry & Gotukola Sambol

Chicken Kothu Rotti

Popular street food prepared by chopping flatbread with eggs, veggies, chicken curry, and spices.

Ingredients:

  • 3 Shredded Parothas
  • 2 Eggs & Chicken Curry Pieces
  • Chopped Onions, Leeks & Cabbage

Sri Lankan Watalappan

A steamed jaggery and coconut milk pudding spiced with nutmeg, cardamom, and cashews.

Ingredients:

  • 250g Dark Kithul Jaggery
  • 5 Whole Eggs
  • 1 cup Thick Coconut Milk & Cashews

Spicy Dhal Vade (Parippu Vade)

Deep-fried crisp tea-time snack made with coarsely ground chana dhal, chili, and fresh curry leaves.

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups Chana Dhal (soaked)
  • Dried Red Chilies & Green Chilies
  • Finely chopped Onions & Curry Leaves
